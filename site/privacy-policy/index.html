Privacy Policy

Location Dots for Android · Effective date: 13 October 2026

In short: Location Dots is designed to keep your location history on your device. The app does not require an account and does not operate a cloud service for your location history. Online map services are used to display maps.

This Privacy Policy explains how Location Dots (“the app”) handles information when you use it. It applies to the Location Dots Android application maintained in the official project repository.

1. Information the app handles

  • Location information: when you grant location permission and enable tracking, the app collects location updates to record your timeline. Depending on your Android permission choices and device settings, this may include precise location and background location.
  • Derived activity and place data: the app processes location updates to identify visits, places, journeys, routes, distances, and timeline summaries.
  • Your edits and settings: saved place names, tracking preferences, appearance settings, and other choices you make in the app.
  • Backups: if you choose to export your data, the app creates a backup for you to save or share using Android's available options.

2. Where your data is stored

Your location history and related app data are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide user accounts or a developer-operated cloud service that uploads or synchronizes your location history. Normal tracking is intended to work without sending your location history to the app developer's servers.

Anyone who can unlock or otherwise access your device may potentially access locally stored information. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock.

3. Location permissions and background tracking

Location permission is needed for location tracking. If you enable background tracking, Android may ask you to grant additional permission or approve system settings. You can change or revoke permissions in Android Settings. Revoking permission or disabling location services can prevent tracking from working.

4. Maps and third-party services

The app uses MapLibre to render maps and OpenFreeMap map resources. Loading a map requires an internet connection. When map resources are requested, the relevant service or infrastructure provider may receive technical request information such as your IP address, request details, and the map tiles or style resources requested. Such requests can reveal an approximate area being viewed. These providers process requests under their own policies and infrastructure arrangements.

The app does not send your complete locally stored timeline to the map provider as part of ordinary tracking. However, map requests themselves are external network requests. Please review the relevant providers' current privacy documentation for details about their processing and retention practices.

5. Sharing and backup files

Location Dots does not upload backups automatically. If you export a backup, choose a sharing destination, or import a file from another location, that action is under your control. Any third-party app or service you use to store or share a backup will handle it under its own terms and privacy policy. Backup files may contain sensitive location history; keep them secure and share them only with people or services you trust.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your history remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app and its app data, subject to Android's behavior and any copies you created elsewhere. You can use the app's available data-management controls to clear locally stored history. Exported backups and copies shared to other apps or services must be deleted separately from those destinations.

7. Advertising, analytics, and sale of data

The app does not operate an advertising profile or sell your location history. This project does not provide a developer-operated account or location-history analytics service. Third-party map infrastructure may process technical information as described above. This policy should be updated if future versions add analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data-processing features.

8. Children's privacy

Location Dots is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect children's information through a developer-operated account service. Parents and guardians should be aware that location history can be sensitive personal information.

9. Security

Keeping history on-device reduces the need to transmit it to a central service, but no software or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Keep Android and the app updated, secure your device, and protect exported backups.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL, with the effective date updated accordingly.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning this policy, open an issue in the Location Dots GitHub repository.

This policy describes the app's intended current behavior. Review it against the exact release build and the data disclosures you submit to Google Play before publishing.
